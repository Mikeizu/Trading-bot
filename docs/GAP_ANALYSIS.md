## Intentional non-claims

No test result demonstrates real profitability, successful live execution, legally available products, calibrated forecast probabilities, fault tolerance under every failure, or broker-side recovery from every ambiguous purchase. Missing evidence is recorded rather than replaced with generated prices or confident wording.
